What Satya doesn’t do

  • It doesn’t replace your assessor or issue the assurance opinion.
  • It doesn’t invent or estimate numbers to fill gaps.
  • It doesn’t file anything with SEBI or the exchanges for you.
  • It flags problems. People decide what to do about them.

Frequently asked questions.

Is BRSR Core mandatory for my company?

BRSR Core assessment or assurance reaches the top 1,000 listed companies by market capitalisation in FY 2026–27. Check SEBI’s latest circulars for your exact obligations.

Does Satya replace my assessor?

No. Assessors still do the independent check. Satya organises the evidence and the review trail so their work moves faster.

We’re a supplier. Do we have to report?

Not directly under BRSR, but listed customers may ask for your data for their value chain disclosures. Satya makes answering quick.

How do we know the numbers are right?

Satya recomputes figures from the source documents and flags anything incomplete or inconsistent. Your assessor remains the independent check.

Which systems does it connect to?

We’re building connectors for tools like Tally, Zoho and SAP. File upload works for everything else.
